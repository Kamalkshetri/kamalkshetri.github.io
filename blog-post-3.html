Staying Connected with Family

Evening Calls to Nepal: Staying Grounded Across the Miles

Read Time: 3 minutes

Building a career and navigating life far from home comes with its challenges, but one daily routine keeps me centered: picking up the phone to call my Mom back in Nepal.

No matter how demanding the workday gets or how busy life becomes in Australia, that daily connection is non-negotiable. Hearing her voice, sharing small updates from my day, and hearing about life back home provides immediate perspective. These conversations serve as a constant reminder of my roots, my family’s support, and the purpose behind my hard work.

In a fast-paced digital world, taking time for real, meaningful conversations across time zones keeps family ties strong and provides grounding no matter where life takes me.

Kamal Mahatara

ICT Systems Support Officer, working toward Cybersecurity Engineering.
