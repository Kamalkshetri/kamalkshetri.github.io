Cricket & Passion for Sports

What Cricket and Virat Kohli Teach Me About Strategy and Resilience

August 2026 · 4 min read

On the surface, cricket and IT support seem worlds apart. One involves leather balls and green fields; the other involves cloud tenants and server logs. Yet, the mindset required for both is surprisingly identical.

Cricket has always been more than just a pastime for me. As a passionate fan of the sport and a huge admirer of Virat Kohli, I draw immense inspiration from his intense work ethic, mental resilience, and relentless pursuit of excellence under pressure. Whether analyzing match situations, anticipating key moments, or staying disciplined through long sessions, cricket demands tactical focus and long-term planning.

Just like troubleshooting a complex enterprise network issue, success in cricket rarely comes from hasty decisions—it comes from execution, systematic adjustment, and composure under pressure. Staying connected to the game recharges my energy while sharpening the analytical focus I bring to my technical work every day.
