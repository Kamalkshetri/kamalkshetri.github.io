Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to things people often ask me. Click a question to expand it.

What do you currently do?

I work as an ICT Systems Support Officer, managing enterprise IT infrastructure, troubleshooting network and system issues, and supporting daily technical operations. Alongside my current role, I am actively specializing in cybersecurity engineering—focusing on network security, threat analysis, and identity and access management (IAM).

Are you open to freelance / job opportunities?

Yes! I am open to full-time roles, contract positions, and select freelance projects, particularly in Cybersecurity Engineering, Network/Systems Administration, and Security Operations (SOC). Feel free to reach out via the Contact Page to discuss opportunities.

What technologies do you specialize in?

Networking & Security: pfSense, Security Onion, Wireshark, Intrusion Detection/Prevention Systems (IDS/IPS), VLAN Segmentation. Systems & IAM: Active Directory (AD DS), Group Policy (GPO), Windows Server 2022, Least Privilege Access/RBAC, Sysmon. Scripting & Automation: PowerShell, Python. Virtualization & Cloud: VMware ESXi, Hyper-V, and basic cloud infrastructure management.

How can I download your résumé?

You can grab it any time from the Home page or the Career page — it's a direct PDF download.

What's the best way to reach you?

The Contact page has a form, my email, and my social links — whichever is easiest for you.

What certifications or security training are you pursuing?

I am actively working through hands-on practical labs and preparing for industry-recognized security credentials, including CompTIA Security+, Network+, and hands-on threat-hunting modules on platforms like TryHackMe and Hack The Box.
